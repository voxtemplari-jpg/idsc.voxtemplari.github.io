Who We Are

Vox Templari is the official student publication of Infotech Development Systems Colleges, Inc. It serves as a platform for student journalism, campus storytelling, commentary, photography, and creative expression.

Through news, features, editorials, opinion pieces, sports coverage, photojournalism, and other forms of campus media, the publication documents the people, events, issues, and experiences that shape the IDSC community.

What We Do

Vox Templari provides students with opportunities to practice responsible journalism while developing their skills in writing, editing, photography, graphic design, layout, multimedia production, and digital publishing.

The publication also maintains an online archive so that important stories from each editorial term remain accessible beyond the tenure of individual student journalists.

Our Commitment

The publication seeks to report accurately, write responsibly, represent the voices of the student community, and preserve significant moments in the life of the institution.
